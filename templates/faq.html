FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Visit the appointment page, choose your service, and submit your preferred date. Our team will confirm the booking.

Can I reschedule my visit?

Yes. Contact our front desk and we’ll help arrange a new consultation time based on doctor availability.

What should I bring to my consultation?

Please bring your ID, insurance information if applicable, referrals, and a list of your current symptoms or medications.

Do you offer emergency eye care?

Yes. We provide urgent support for eye injuries, sudden vision changes, severe pain, and acute concerns.
